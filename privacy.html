MERITHAI / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Information processed

When you use MerithAI through Discord, the application may process your Discord user ID, username, server and channel identifiers, messages or voice content sent to it, command and interaction metadata, and technical diagnostics required to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

This information is used to provide responses and commands, maintain requested conversation context, deliver voice features, troubleshoot failures, enforce access controls, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Storage and retention

Operational logs, transcripts, and conversation data may be stored on the developer's systems for service operation, debugging, and replay of requested features. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, legal obligations, or resolving disputes.

4. Third-party processing

Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting, speech, model, or other providers needed to deliver a feature you request. MerithAI does not sell personal information.

5. Your choices

Server administrators can remove MerithAI from a server. You can stop sending messages or voice input. You may request deletion of identifiable MerithAI records by contacting MichaelBTryin@proton.me. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the service and its data. No internet service is completely secure, and use of MerithAI is at your own risk.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when MerithAI or its data practices change. The updated date above will be changed when revisions are published. Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to MichaelBTryin@proton.me.
